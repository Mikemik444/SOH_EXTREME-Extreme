0.11.16: Fishing NPC prerequisites and walk-through bush parity.
The fishing rod action requires NPC Soul, the configured Hylian/shared Speak,
a wallet and the configured Fish/Animal Soul, in addition to the existing rod,
region and age requirements. Bottled overworld fish do not require an NPC.
En_Wood02 bush checks require their soul and actual region/age access, not a
cutting weapon, Grab or Roll. En_Kusa grass retains real interaction methods.
Native patch separates Fishing owner rendering/dialog from pond environment,
and fish visibility from NPC Soul. Fish can appear on in-room soul receipt.
No IDs, locations, received-item indexes or offspring exclusions were changed.
