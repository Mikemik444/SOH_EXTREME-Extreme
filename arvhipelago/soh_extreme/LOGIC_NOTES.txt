SOH-EXTREME APWorld 0.6.1 - full-settings interaction audit

Rule composition policy
-----------------------
EXTREME settings stack. A shuffled Soul/ability never replaces the normal
physical requirements, and one physical method never replaces the other valid
methods. In logical terms:

    enabled category gate(s) AND (valid method A OR valid method B OR ...)

The APWorld uses Archipelago-SoH's age-aware LogicHelpers for stock items so
owning an adult-only/child-only item does not automatically mean Link can use it.

Audited physical families
-------------------------
Pots:
  Pot Soul, when shuffled, AND one valid close-pot method:
  innate/custom Grab, usable swords/sticks, Megaton Hammer, Bombs/Bombchus,
  Boomerang, Hookshot/Longshot, Slingshot/Bow. Giant's Knife is adult-only.

Small crates:
  Crate Soul, when shuffled, AND (jumpslash OR explosives OR Grab).
Large crates:
  Crate Soul only; their stock/native break behavior remains unchanged.

Rocks:
  Rock/Boulder Soul, when shuffled, AND (BlastOrSmash OR Grab).
  Underwater rocks use their stricter Bombchu/adult-Grab plus water-access rule.

Boulders:
  Rock/Boulder Soul, when shuffled, AND class-specific interaction:
  generic = explosives OR Hammer; bronze = Hammer; silver = Silver Gauntlets.

Bush/grass:
  Grass/Bush Soul, when shuffled, AND stock CanCutShrubs methods.
  Native Giant's Knife adult route is also retained for fork-native bushes.

Trees:
  Tree Soul, when shuffled, AND Roll when Roll is shuffled.

Beehives:
  Beehive Soul stacks on stock upper/lower hive rules. Stock rules preserve
  Hookshot/Boomerang, Bombchu Beehive trick, Sling/Bow option and lower-hive
  Bomb Bag behavior.

Red ice:
  Blue Fire logic, including Ice Arrows only when Blue Fire Arrows is enabled.

Icicles/stalagmites:
  Falling trigger icicles remain region-triggered. Stalagmites require
  jumpslash/explosives/Giant's Knife according to native logic.

Signs / Skulltulas / animals / NPCs / scrubs:
  Their selected category Souls stack independently with stock reachability,
  Speak, Enemy Soul, NPC Soul and other enabled interaction settings.

Wonder Items:
  119 fork-native Wonder checks were audited against the native C++ LOCATION
  expressions. Common child/adult, Bow/Slingshot, water, Hookshot, Hover Boots,
  Epona-song and Market day/night requirements are now represented instead of
  treating the broad parent region as the full rule. A native trick-only check
  that has no AP-exposed equivalent is not allowed to become AP progression.

Multiworld placement
--------------------
No EXTREME progression item is forced local or forced into starting/early
inventory. Normal Archipelago fill may place Roll, Grab, Climb, Crawl, Speak,
Open Chest, Souls, Shovel, Flow of Time, etc. in other players' worlds, subject
to the corrected reachability rules.

Existing 0.5.x/0.6.0 safety work retained
-----------------------------------------
Per-save AP received-item index + ARCHIPELAGO save marker, title-screen gameplay
queue safety, in-game AP chat, DeathLink, TrapLink, working trap application,
freestanding AP reporting, lazy location resolution/chunked scouting, persistent
bush/rock sanity, hidden-grotto Shovel gating, Flow-of-Time save recalculation,
and Kakariko child/adult collision correction.

0.6.5 corrections
-----------------
- Souls remain existential gates: their actor category is absent until the Soul is received.
- Grass/Bush Soul now removes bush actors too.
- ZR Near Domain Freestanding PoH no longer accepts Bunny Hood alone as a no-Cucco route.
- AP-received ordinary SoH items with a valid vanilla ItemID fall back to Item_Give instead of being discarded/asserted.

0.6.6 Animal Soul generation audit
----------------------------------
Animal Soul remains an existence gate.  AP generation now explicitly blocks:
- every stock cow reward in the native fork,
- Zora River frog checks,
- Talon's Chickens and child Anju,
- direct pond/grotto/puddle fish checks and fishing,
- Epona-dependent race/time-trial/Big-Poe/Horseback-Archery checks,
until Animal Soul exists.

This closes the 0.6.5 failure where AP placed Animal Soul in Forest Temple but
still used cow rewards in the playthrough before Animal Soul was collected.

0.6.8 traversal-route correction
--------------------------------
- GV Waterfall Freestanding PoH: child direct route now requires Animal Soul + Grab (Cucco), while swim/adult alternatives remain possible through their normal region rules.
- ZR Near Open Grotto Freestanding PoH: child Grab routes to the pillar/upper area now require Animal Soul; Bronze Scale, Hovers, Boomerang/adult alternatives remain intact where native logic allows them.
- Kak Impas House Freestanding PoH: child daytime Grab route to Impa's ledge now requires Animal Soul because it is a Cucco route. Hookshot/adult/trick routes remain governed by the stock/native rules.

0.6.9 AP receive-path fix
-------------------------
- Vanilla-backed AP rewards no longer pass through Randomizer_Item_Give, which rejects MOD_NONE entries.
- Gold Skulltula Token, Piece of Heart (including WINNER), and Heart Container are applied explicitly with Item_Give.
- All other non-major vanilla-backed AP rewards also use Item_Give; true MOD_RANDOMIZER rewards still use Randomizer_Item_Give.

0.7.1 stock event Soul audit
----------------------------
- GC Woods Warp / GC Woods Warp From Woods require Rock/Boulder Soul when
  rocks/boulders are shuffled. Bombchus alone cannot set the shortcut event
  while the obstruction actor is absent.
- Stock fish and bug availability helper events require Animal Soul.
- Bug Rock helpers also require Rock/Boulder Soul; Bug Grass/Shrub helpers
  require Grass/Bush Soul.
- Deku-Baba Can Farm Sticks/Nuts helpers require Enemy Soul.
- Pot-based Can Farm Sticks/Nuts helpers require Pot Soul.

These rules affect Archipelago generation/playthrough logic, so make a new seed.

0.7.2 Lost Woods / SFM traversal audit
--------------------------------------
- Fork-native Lost Woods and Sacred Forest Meadow locations now require a real
  route into Lost Woods when Climb is shuffled.
- Supported alternate routes: Climb, Hookshot, opened GC woods warp, or the
  Zora's River shortcut via Silver Scale / Iron Boots.
- SFM additionally requires Child access through the corridor or Saria's Song
  for the adult route.
- Do NOT blanket-gate every location whose display name contains "Grass".
  Native source confirms checks such as KF North Grass East Rupee are separate
  freestanding actors, and some Wonder checks merely use grass as a landmark.

0.7.3 region-level traversal logic pass
---------------------------------------
- Lost Woods shuffled-Climb access is now applied to every location/event whose
  parent region is Lost Woods, including inherited stock AP events.
- GC Woods Warp From Woods cannot bootstrap itself through the already-open GC
  shortcut; it must be reached from KF/Hookshot/ZR alternatives first.
- Sacred Forest Meadow now uses a physical route expression: Minuet direct warp,
  or Lost Woods access plus the appropriate Mido corridor condition.
- Child corridor requires the Showed Mido the Sword and Shield event; adult
  corridor requires Saria's Song.

This changes generation logic; generate a new seed.


0.7.4 structural entrance-graph logic
-------------------------------------
The important change is architectural:
- movement shuffles are attached to actual Region entrances;
- downstream stock checks, EXTREME checks and event locations inherit them;
- GC Woods Warp From Woods can no longer become reachable merely because its
  own local item rule is satisfied while the player cannot enter Lost Woods;
- KF -> Lost Woods uses the native fork route:
    Climb OR Hookshot OR the inherited Adult bean/trick route;
- native mandatory Crawl entrances are gated at the graph level;
- several mandatory Climb and Speak transitions are also graph-gated;
- removed the incorrect 0.7.3 assumption that child SFM access needs the
  Kokiri-forest "Showed Mido the Sword and Shield" event.

Location-level Soul gates remain in addition to these entrance rules because
Soul disappearance affects the actor/check itself rather than only traversal.


0.7.5 generated native-logic audit
----------------------------------
- Replaced hand-maintained custom entrance tables with an auto-generated table
  extracted from every native C++ ENTRANCE macro.
- The generated table contains 308 unique custom-action entrance edges.
- Native graph contraction resolves fork-only intermediate regions (including
  KF Outside Lost Woods) onto the actual stock AP region graph.
- Kokiri Forest -> Lost Woods has a hard fail-safe Climb invariant: if the AP
  entrance cannot be found, generation aborts instead of producing a bad seed.
- Generated exact stock-location rules are keyed by the same AP numeric IDs used
  by the native Archipelago client.
- Central native helper semantics are included: Enemy Soul, Pot/Crate/Grass/Rock/
  Tree/Beehive/Sign/Skulltula Souls, Shovel, NPC/Speak, Business Scrub Soul, etc.
- EVENT_ACCESS dependencies are conservatively applied to stock event locations
  in their source region so helper events cannot bypass the custom physical rules.
- Existing fork-native family rules and Soul disappearance logic are retained.
- The overlay is intentionally conservative: it can delay a check that has a
  legitimate alternate route, but it must not certify a route the game cannot do.


0.7.6 boolean-preserving native logic
---------------------------------------
- Fixes 0.7.5 FillError caused by flattening native OR alternatives into AND requirements.
- Generated NativeLogicAlternatives.py preserves custom requirements as DNF: AND within a branch, OR across branches.
- AP entrance overlays are now derived from native paths through contracted regions instead of fallback-smearing requirements over all target/source edges.
- Removed the 0.7.5 region-wide union of EVENT_ACCESS requirements; exact stock progression-event gates remain below.
- Kokiri Forest -> Lost Woods is explicitly modeled as Climb OR Hookshot OR Adult+KF Bean Planted, preventing early LW/SFM while keeping real alternate routes.
- Exact native locations now receive an OR-preserving rule rather than every discovered helper/action as an AND.
- Manifest format lowered to 7 for Archipelago 0.6.7 compatibility.


0.7.7 Archipelago 0.6.7 unresolved Rule compatibility

- No reachability requirements were changed from 0.7.6.
