SOH-EXTREME APWorld 0.11.22 - source-only logic update

Based on the last-working extracted APWorld in arvhipelago/ at GitHub commit
473e6c69d146fb426fd83f9d1278c3c717fa865d. Its 101 files match the supplied
0.11.21d APWorld exactly (Git tree a19193300a57f3572180e8a8a9ce037c1c3b403d).

New APWorld logic:
- Dodongos Cavern Boss Region -> Boss Entryway calls can_grab(bundle), rather
  than True_(). Both child and adult routes must satisfy the block/switch action.
  Grab is innate when Shuffle Grab is off; otherwise Grab / Power Bracelet is
  required. Other chest/boss/room requirements are unchanged.
- Native DODONGOS_CAVERN_BOSS_AREA is aliased to the AP enum
  DODONGOS_CAVERN_BOSS_REGION in BOTH create_regions and set_rules, preserving
  the native Grab condition during region placement and rule projection.
- The archive and internal manifests, plus the Python tracker transport, are
  0.11.22, matching the C++ tracker from the already-delivered 0.11.22 soul patch.

Preserved, not newly added:
- The 0.11.21d house-pot restriction that excludes unusable indoor explosives.
- Existing enemy-room, individual soul, ability, progressive item, time,
  grotto, silver-rupee, trap and slot-data code from the last-working world.
- All item/location IDs and the already-modified _vendor_oot_soh package.

This APWorld contains NO new soul graphics. Graphics live in the game client.
No stock helper downloads or replacements are needed. Zip the CONTENTS of
arvhipelago (archipelago.json and soh_extreme/), not the outer directory.
Restart Archipelago/Universal Tracker after replacing the installed APWorld.

Existing seed placements are not changed by updating the rules. Generation
with the new rules is required for newly placed rewards to respect this gate.
An old seed with a circular placement is not repaired by this source update.

Focused source/rule tests and syntax checks are provided outside the package.
They are not an exhaustive native/AP parity audit or a full seed-generation test.
