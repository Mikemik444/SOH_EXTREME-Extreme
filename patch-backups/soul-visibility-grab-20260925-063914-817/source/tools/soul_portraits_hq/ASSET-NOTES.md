# Enemy soul portrait assets

The icons are deterministic compositions of the provided horned `gBossSoul` texture
and the enemy portrait images supplied in this conversation. The full Stalfos image
is used where available; the remaining portraits are extracted from the provided
contact sheet. Background transparency, sizing, and a shared subtle edge are applied.
Dark Link receives a brightness adjustment and the tentacle is angled for readability.
No new live enemy models or object banks are used. No ownership or licensing claim
over the original game artwork is implied by this patch.

The source images and compositor are included to make the edits inspectable and
repeatable. No font files or original full-game resource archives are included.
