SOH-EXTREME 0.11.15 — Lake Hylia, song completion and reachability caches

Requires the existing 0.11.14 native patch plus the accompanying native changes.
- Hyrule Field lake fence requires Climb or actually usable adult Epona.
- River/underwater shore connections require Swim, not Iron Boots, hearts or a Fairy void.
- Warp hubs and SFM warp alternatives require playable songs, not song ownership.
- Playing a song requires its complete individual-note group in note mode, an Ocarina,
  and its distinct required buttons. Duplicate notes/buttons cannot substitute.
- Whole-song prefill modes do not place full songs while individual notes are enabled;
  74 distinct notes use ordinary locations. Starting songs grant their complete note groups.
- AP common starting inventory and from-pool requests are expanded before Main uses them.
- Successful removal invalidates both child/adult region caches, not only a stale flag.
- Scarecrow always needs an instrument and two distinct buttons, including taught-song mode.

No enemy location IDs, receipt slots or offspring exclusions changed.
See the companion archive VALIDATION.md for executed tests and limits. This is not
certification of all game geometry, Master Quest, all option combinations or all seeds.
