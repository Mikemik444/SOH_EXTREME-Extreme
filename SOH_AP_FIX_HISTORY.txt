FROZEN DUSK / LON LON RANCH GUAYS (0.11.32)
- User reported nighttime Ranch Guays in logic while stuck just before night,
  without Flow of Time, Sun's Song, an Ocarina or all Ocarina buttons.
- Read-only inspection of the running game's configuration confirmed shuffled
  Flow of Time and FrozenStartingTime=3 (Dusk). No game files were changed.
- The runtime freezes Dusk at 0xB555. The engine sets night only when the clock
  is greater than 0xC000 or less than 0x4555. Dusk is therefore still daytime.
  AP rules and native generation reset logic incorrectly grouped Dusk with Night.
- Corrected enemy spawn-time rules, shared AP day/night helpers, Market wonder
  gates/night-name fallbacks and both native region reset paths. Dawn, Day and
  Dusk now count as day; only the Night preset starts at night.
- All 15 Ranch Guays remain child-night checks with the same IDs. They require
  nighttime access, their soul and a usable weapon. They stay out of logic at
  frozen dusk without Flow, even if other progression or song items are owned.
  A true Night start does not need Flow to collect its already available Guays.
  With Flow received, normal waiting can reach night without song equipment.
- Runtime clock values, item receipts and save contents are unchanged. This
  corrects the tracker for existing dusk slots but does not relocate old items.
  Generate new multiworlds with 0.11.32 for corrected placement rules. Existing
  0.11.30/earlier worlds also retain the previous grotto/other placement defects.
- Verification: old rules failed 34 dusk assertions; corrected AP/UT suite has
  680 passing assertions across Dawn/Day/Dusk/Night; 105 compiled engine clock
  and native reset assertions; 36 mandatory-time checks; Dusk and Night seed
  generation/replay through all 3319 checks and the goal. No live playthrough.

VALIDATION LIMITS
Passing generation establishes consistency under the encoded rules. It cannot
prove those rules capture every physical obstacle in the game. No connected
in-game playthrough has been performed by these automated checks, and not every
settings combination is certified. Continue recording concrete counterexamples
like the screenshots, then test both missing-item cases and valid alternatives.
