PURPOSE
This file records the reports and decisions from this repair conversation so
future work can refer to them. It is project documentation, not a promise of
permanent assistant memory across chats. Keep it with the source and bring it
into any new repair conversation. New reports should become regression cases.

NPC SPEECH SANITY REPAIR (0.11.29)
- Intended behavior: first conversation with each NPC is one AP check; subsequent
  conversations behave normally. A character who moves or changes models shares
  one identity. A shopkeeper is one conversation, independent of the eight shelves.
- User talked to all Kokiri without any speech checks being sent. The supplied
  server log confirms Speak Kokiri and NPC Soul were received; ordinary checks
  worked. AP_32037442678715337267 slot 4 had npc_speech_sanity=1 and the correct
  native NpcSpeechSanity=1. The option was linked; the 114-entry reward-based
  location map omitted ordinary Kokiri. Its disabled generic bank had no checks.
- New seeds use identity version 2 and a finite 181-conversation catalogue,
  including all 13 Kokiri child identities, Mido, Saria and the shopkeeper.
  Grog's child/adult models share an identity. The Skull Kid duet uses its actual
  conversation controller. Capturing Gerudo sentries, the transient membership-
  card giver and cinematic scenery are outside the current catalogue. This is
  finite conversation coverage, not every possible dialogue actor. No anonymous
  capacity checks are added.
- Player conversation hooks now receive the selected actor, including C-Up.
  Script-started textboxes are observed as well. Queued and server-confirmed
  checks both stop duplicate sends. Normal dialogue resumes even before the
  acknowledgment, and automatic dialogue state machines are left running.
- Manual first-talk checks require NPC Soul and the specific language when
  enabled. Physical region/age/time rules govern AP and UT. Conversation rules
  do not require buying the stock or earning the NPC's normal reward. Named
  moving/story NPCs can use conservative guaranteed routes in the tracker.
- Sheik's normal dialogue and the Mask Shop guest remain available until their
  version-2 conversation is checked, preventing their story despawn from losing
  those checks. Old seeds/native games retain the original despawn behavior.
- The textbox observer verifies the actor is still in the live scene list before
  dereferencing it; stale dialogue pointers and Navi enemy descriptions are ignored.
- Legacy seed IDs are not remapped. UT can reconstruct their original 114 speech
  locations. The supplied existing seed cannot gain nonexistent Kokiri locations
  from a client update: install the matching APWorld and generate a NEW multiworld.
- Validation: 215 compiled C++ assertions, 127 AP/UT/slot assertions, full day/night
  generation replays, and 181 fresh inventories across 3,325 configured AP checks
  (601,825 AP-versus-UT comparisons). No connected in-game playthrough performed.

ALL ABILITIES / SOULS / TIME AUDIT (0.11.28)
- User explicitly requires every shuffled capability to affect logic, not just
  Grab and Climb. Checks stay available for normal item placement, and become
  in logic only when an actual route and interaction are possible.
- Scope includes Roll, Grab/Strength, Climb, Crawl, Swim/Scales, speech and every
  language, chest opening, Shovel, Flow of Time, fishing pole, stick/nut bags,
  ocarina/buttons/song notes, wallets, and enemy/boss/animal/object/NPC/bean souls.
- Gerudo Valley Crate Ledge child route now requires Cucco Soul (or shared
  Animal Soul) plus Grab in native and AP logic. A reachable adult with Longshot
  remains a valid alternative. Both crate and contained heart piece share it.
- Hyrule Field drawbridge Wonder Items 1-3 require child Link and reachable day.
  Frozen night without Flow of Time no longer passes. Normal child drawbridge
  entry also needs day; genuine alternate entrances are retained.
- Crates already require Crate Soul AND a valid action: large crates accept
  Roll, explosives or usable Hammer; small crates also accept appropriate
  jumpslash/Grab. Soul ownership by itself never supplies an interaction.
- All 84 native soul entries are already major items with long hold-up animation
  and advancement metadata. AP boss souls now also reject caller attempts to
  downgrade them to filler, matching the other soul items' progression status.
- Latest YAML snapshot: validation/fixtures/ap_latest_settings_20260929.yaml.
  It supersedes the old spoiler fixture for this audit, not the old seed's
  already resolved settings. There are 3,266 candidate network locations before
  prefill; eight become vanilla shop-stock events, leaving 3,258 AP checks.
- Every final check was evaluated with 181 fresh inventory scenarios, removing
  capability receipts before collecting/sweeping events. 589,698 AP/installed-UT
  comparisons passed, alongside source prerequisite/action/age/time/equipment
  tests, all-setting slot reconstruction and compiled native route tests.
- The automated source crosswalk explicitly retains ambiguous region mappings
  and untested physical encounters for review. It is not a physical playthrough
  or proof that all possible settings combinations are correct.

ZORA RIVER REQUIREMENTS (0.11.27, retained)
- Wonder Near Cucco 1-3: child Link, access to that river section, and Swim.
  With Swim shuffled, physical Progressive Scale #1 grants Swim/Bronze Scale.
  Being on the bank, carrying a Cucco, or owning adult equipment is insufficient.
- Upper Circle Boulder and Upper Circle Rock 1-8: Climb AND either
  (child + usable Cucco + Grab) OR actual adult access. Climb is required on
  BOTH routes. Merely owning adult equipment does not unlock adult age.
- A usable Cucco needs Cucco Soul in individual-animal mode, Animal Soul in
  shared mode, and no soul item when animal souls are not shuffled. Grab and
  Climb likewise become innate only when their own shuffles are disabled.
- Existing object interaction and Rock / Boulder Soul requirements remain.
  Names and network IDs stay stable. Apply these rules to native Check Finder,
  the AP generator and Universal Tracker, not just the displayed list.
- Tests: validation/test_zr_routes.py (actual AP/UT receipt matrix),
  validation/test_zr_native.py (compiled production location predicates).

  Sword and Deku Shield, plus NPC Soul and Kokiri speech when those are shuffled.
  Individual speech means Speak Kokiri; another language is not sufficient.
- The five child Withered Deku Babas in Kokiri Forest Room 1 are BEYOND Mido.
  Their native/AP region is KF Outside Deku Tree, not the main forest.

- Tests: validation/test_mido_routes.py, test_mido_native.py, test_deku_logic.py,

  validation/test_waterfall_parent.py.

SETTINGS / OLD SEED CAVEAT
The earlier pasted YAML and the later supplied spoiler are different settings.
The previous Zora River release used the 216 resolved settings reconstructed in
validation/fixtures/ap_mido_seed_settings_20260929.yaml. That older seed enables
Closed Forest, shuffled abilities, individual language/animal/enemy souls and
NPC Soul. Triforce Hunt uses 60 pieces with 50 percent required (30 pieces).

The supplied AP_99430200357528780636 multiworld was generated with older rules.
Correcting logic does not relocate already placed items. With corrected Mido
and ZR rules, offline replay stalls after 33 checks; an extra Climb alone reaches
355 checks but still stalls. An extra Climb plus one physical Progressive Scale
reaches the goal and all 3,258 checks in the replay, assuming eventual delivery
of all 39 SOH items placed in the other game. The other game's logic is not run.
No server messages, item grants or save modifications were performed here.
That recovery was tested against 0.11.27. It was not revalidated against the
additional 0.11.28 gates and is not a guarantee for the new rules. Existing item
placements do not change when updating. Regenerate with the matching APWorld
for the new logic to govern placement. No recovery commands were sent.

VALIDATION LIMITS
Passing generation establishes consistency under the encoded rules. It cannot
prove those rules capture every physical obstacle in the game. No connected
in-game playthrough has been performed by these automated checks, and not every
settings combination is certified. Continue recording concrete counterexamples
like the screenshots, then test both missing-item cases and valid alternatives.
