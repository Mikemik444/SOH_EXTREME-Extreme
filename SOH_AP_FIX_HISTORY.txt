Updated 2026-09-29 for cumulative release 0.11.27

PURPOSE
This file records the reports and decisions from this repair conversation so
future work can refer to them. It is project documentation, not a promise of
permanent assistant memory across chats. Keep it with the source and bring it
into any new repair conversation. New reports should become regression cases.

CURRENT ZORA RIVER REQUIREMENTS (0.11.27)
- Wonder Near Cucco 1-3: child Link, access to that river section, and Swim.
  With Swim shuffled, physical Progressive Scale #1 grants Swim/Bronze Scale.
  Being on the bank, carrying a Cucco, or owning adult equipment is insufficient.
- Upper Circle Boulder and Upper Circle Rock 1-8: Climb AND either
  (child + usable Cucco + Grab) OR actual adult access. Climb is required on
  BOTH routes. Merely owning adult equipment does not unlock adult age.
- A usable Cucco needs Cucco Soul in individual-animal mode, Animal Soul in
  shared mode, and no soul item when animal souls are not shuffled. Grab and
  Climb likewise become innate only when their own shuffles are disabled.
- Existing object interaction and Rock / Boulder Soul requirements remain.
  Names and network IDs stay stable. Apply these rules to native Check Finder,
  the AP generator and Universal Tracker, not just the displayed list.
- Tests: validation/test_zr_routes.py (actual AP/UT receipt matrix),
  validation/test_zr_native.py (compiled production location predicates).

GRASS / STRENGTH (0.11.25)
- With Grab shuffled, Strength #1 supplies basic Grab / Power Bracelet.
  It permits appropriate small-object interactions, not lifting grass.
- Strength #2 supplies Goron's Bracelet and permits lifting grass. Without
  Grab shuffle, the normal first Strength supplies Goron's Bracelet.
- Valid cutting methods remain alternatives. Sticks alone do not cut grass.
- Tests: validation/test_grass_strength.py, validation/test_grass_native.py.

MIDO, DEKU SCRUBS AND BABAS (0.11.26 and earlier)
- Closed Forest On or Deku Only: before Mido moves, his path requires Kokiri
  Sword and Deku Shield, plus NPC Soul and Kokiri speech when those are shuffled.
  Individual speech means Speak Kokiri; another language is not sufficient.
- The five child Withered Deku Babas in Kokiri Forest Room 1 are BEYOND Mido.
  Their native/AP region is KF Outside Deku Tree, not the main forest.
- Closed Forest Off keeps the open path. Enemy soul and combat gates still apply.
- Solid upright/spinning Withered Babas require a usable sword or boomerang;
  Deku Sticks are not a valid kill method for these actors.
- Deku Tree scrub barriers need a shield that can reflect nuts. Live finder
  state must reflect owned equipment, not a future shop purchase event.
- AP-managed settings disable the stale refill-only shield/tunic purchase
  restriction. Vanilla stock prices remain separate from randomized slot prices.
- Tests: validation/test_mido_routes.py, test_mido_native.py, test_deku_logic.py,
  test_deku_native.py. Gerudo Valley adult waterfall parent regression:
  validation/test_waterfall_parent.py.

NETWORK / ITEM PRESENTATION (0.11.24 and earlier)
- Network > Archipelago includes the server's hint list, filtering and found state.
- Scrub/merchant AP item descriptions should show the item rather than No Hint.
- Important/useful remote AP rewards queue a named pickup textbox and hold-up
  presentation. Fillers retain quick notifications. Safe animation gates remain.
- Received-item handling has priority to reduce avoidable delays; this does not
  promise zero network latency. Queue/save/connection safety fixes are retained.
- AP name/control-character handling and PrintJSON player-ID parsing were hardened.
- Tests: validation/test_ap_presentation.py, test_ap_hints_protocol.cpp,
  test_ap_slot_contract.py, test_ap_delivery_audit.py and native delivery tests.

SETTINGS / OLD SEED CAVEAT
The earlier pasted YAML and the later supplied spoiler are different settings.
For the current reports, use the 216 resolved settings reconstructed in
validation/fixtures/ap_mido_seed_settings_20260929.yaml. That seed enables
Closed Forest, shuffled abilities, individual language/animal/enemy souls and
NPC Soul. Triforce Hunt uses 60 pieces with 50 percent required (30 pieces).

The supplied AP_99430200357528780636 multiworld was generated with older rules.
Correcting logic does not relocate already placed items. With corrected Mido
and ZR rules, offline replay stalls after 33 checks; an extra Climb alone reaches
355 checks but still stalls. An extra Climb plus one physical Progressive Scale
reaches the goal and all 3,258 checks in the replay, assuming eventual delivery
of all 39 SOH items placed in the other game. The other game's logic is not run.
No server messages, item grants or save modifications were performed here.
Regenerate with the updated APWorld, or use the explicit host recovery steps
in README-ZORA-RIVER.txt if keeping that old seed.

VALIDATION LIMITS
Passing generation establishes consistency under the encoded rules. It cannot
prove those rules capture every physical obstacle in the game. No connected
in-game playthrough has been performed by these automated checks, and not every
settings combination is certified. Continue recording concrete counterexamples
like the screenshots, then test both missing-item cases and valid alternatives.
