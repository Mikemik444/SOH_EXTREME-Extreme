0.11.21d indoor-pot native requirement

Each targeted house pot changes from:
    logic->CanBreakPots()

to:
    logic->CanBreakPots() &&
    (logic->HasItem(RG_POWER_BRACELET) ||
     logic->CanJumpslash() ||
     logic->CanUse(RG_GIANTS_KNIFE) ||
     logic->CanUse(RG_BOOMERANG) ||
     logic->CanUse(RG_HOOKSHOT) ||
     logic->CanUse(RG_LONGSHOT) ||
     logic->CanUse(RG_FAIRY_SLINGSHOT) ||
     logic->CanUse(RG_FAIRY_BOW))

The first term keeps Pot Soul and all normal pot gating. The second term prevents
Bomb/Bombchu-only inventories from satisfying residential interior pots.
